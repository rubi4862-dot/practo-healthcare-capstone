# Appointment Booking Policy

Patients can book an in-clinic or online consultation through the Practo app or website up to 30 days in advance. Each booking is confirmed instantly with an appointment ID in the format APT-XXXX, sent by SMS and in-app notification. A patient may hold at most three upcoming appointments at the same time across all specialties. Walk-in patients are seen only if a slot is free, and booked patients always take priority.
