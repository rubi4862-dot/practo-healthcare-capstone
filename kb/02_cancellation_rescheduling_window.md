# Cancellation and Rescheduling Window

Appointments can be cancelled free of charge up to 4 hours before the scheduled start time, and the full consultation fee is refunded to the original payment method within 5-7 working days. Cancellations made less than 4 hours before the slot receive a 50% refund. An appointment can be rescheduled at no cost up to two times, as long as the change is made at least 2 hours before the original slot. A patient who does not show up without cancelling is marked as a No-Show and is not eligible for a refund.
