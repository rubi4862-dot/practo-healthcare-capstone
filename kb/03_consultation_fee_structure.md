# Consultation Fee Structure by Specialty

Consultation fees are set per specialty and are shown before the patient confirms a booking. General Medicine consultations cost between INR 300 and INR 600, Pediatrics between INR 400 and INR 800, and Dermatology between INR 500 and INR 1000. Orthopedics consultations range from INR 600 to INR 1200, and Cardiology consultations range from INR 800 to INR 1500. Fees include GST and do not cover lab tests, procedures, or medicines, which are billed separately.
