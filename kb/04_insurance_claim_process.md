# Insurance Claim Process

Patients with health insurance pay the consultation fee upfront and then claim reimbursement from their insurer. After the visit, the patient can download an itemised invoice and the doctor's consultation note from the Practo app to attach to the claim. Cashless claims are supported only at partner hospitals for procedures and admissions, not for routine outpatient consultations. Practo does not decide whether a claim is approved; that decision rests with the patient's insurance provider.
