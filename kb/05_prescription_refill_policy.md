# Prescription Refill Policy

A prescription refill can be requested through the app for ongoing medicines that the same doctor prescribed within the last 90 days. The doctor reviews each refill request within 24 hours and may approve it, ask for a follow-up visit, or decline it. Refills are never issued for controlled or scheduled drugs, which always require a fresh consultation. Approved refills appear as a digital prescription that the patient can use at any pharmacy.
