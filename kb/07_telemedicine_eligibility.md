# Telemedicine Eligibility

Online video consultations are available for General Medicine, Dermatology, and Pediatrics, and for follow-ups in any specialty. A first Cardiology or Orthopedics consultation must be in person because it usually needs a physical examination or imaging. Patients need a stable internet connection and a device with a camera for a video consultation. Telemedicine is not suitable for emergencies, and the doctor may ask the patient to visit a clinic if a physical examination is needed.
