# Follow-up Visit Discount Policy

A follow-up visit with the same doctor within 7 days of the original consultation is free of charge. A follow-up booked between 8 and 14 days after the original visit gets a 50% discount on the consultation fee. After 14 days, the full fee applies because the visit is treated as a new consultation. The discount applies only when the doctor marked follow-up as required on the original appointment.
