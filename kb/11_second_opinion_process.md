# Second Opinion Process

Patients can request a second opinion from another doctor in the same specialty by booking a standard consultation and choosing "Second opinion" as the visit reason. The patient should upload previous reports, scans, and prescriptions at least 24 hours before the appointment so the doctor can review them. A second-opinion consultation is charged at the normal fee for that specialty. The second doctor gives an independent assessment and does not contact the first doctor unless the patient asks.
