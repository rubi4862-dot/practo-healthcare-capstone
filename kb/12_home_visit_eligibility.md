# Home Visit Eligibility

Home visits are available for patients over 65 years old, patients with limited mobility, and children under 2 years old, within 10 km of a partner clinic. Home visits cover General Medicine and Pediatrics only and must be booked at least 12 hours in advance. A home-visit surcharge of INR 500 is added on top of the regular consultation fee. Home visits are not available for emergencies or for procedures that need clinic equipment.
